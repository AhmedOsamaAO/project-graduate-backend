# Faculty Postgraduate Records

Records managed by one faculty's postgraduate office within one university.

## Language

**Postgraduate office**:
The faculty office that maintains students' postgraduate academic records and confirms the rules governing them.

**Staff user**:
A person authorized to operate the postgraduate records system. Students and academic supervisors are not application users in the MVP.

**Qualification**:
An awarded degree recorded in a student's educational history, distinct from an enrollment in a postgraduate program. Its awarding institution and any faculty or department describe where that degree was earned.
_Avoid_: Student-level degree summary

**Enrollment**:
A student's official registration in a postgraduate program in the operating faculty, distinct from an application or an awarded qualification.

**Qualifying qualification**:
The awarded qualification recorded as the basis for admission to an enrollment. It belongs to the enrolled student and is distinct from any qualification later awarded for completing that enrollment.

**Official enrollment number**:
The office-issued identifier entered on an enrollment record, preserving its written form. It is not assumed to uniquely identify a registration across all years and tracks.

**Archived student record**:
A student record excluded from ordinary lists because it was entered in error or is no longer actively managed, while its history is preserved. Archiving is distinct from completing or cancelling an enrollment.

**Professional track**:
The faculty's postgraduate track without master's or doctoral subdivisions, distinct from its academic track of master's and PhD study.

**Equivalency status**:
The office's recorded assessment of whether an awarded qualification requires equivalency and, when applicable, the progress or outcome of that assessment. An unassessed qualification is distinct from one confirmed not to require equivalency.

**Archived qualification**:
A qualification excluded from ordinary lists and enrollment eligibility while its record and supporting documents are preserved. Its archive state is independent of whether the student's record is archived.

**Internal supervisor**:
A supervisor belonging to the faculty operating the system. A supervisor from another faculty, including one in the same university, is external.

**External supervisor**:
A supervisor outside the operating faculty whose approval is assessed by staff using supporting documents. Approval belongs to the supervisor rather than to an individual thesis appointment.

**Academic supervisor**:
The internal supervisor assigned to a professional enrollment, distinct from a thesis supervisor.

**Thesis supervisor**:
A member of the current supervision team for a registered master’s or PhD thesis, serving as main supervisor or co-supervisor.

**Main supervisor**:
The single internal professor or associate professor leading a thesis supervision team.

**Withdrawn thesis**:
A registered thesis record set aside as mistaken while remaining preserved. It is distinct from a cancelled enrollment and does not prevent registering a replacement thesis for that enrollment.
